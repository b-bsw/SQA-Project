About the numbers

How results are counted

Each CSV row is one recorded result for a target in a round. Revealing rate is revealing results divided by all recorded results, including unavailable ones. Line and branch coverage are the arithmetic averages of recorded per-result percentages; missing values are excluded. Different methods and rounds have different result counts, so compare them with that coverage in mind.
